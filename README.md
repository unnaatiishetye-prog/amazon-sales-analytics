# Amazon Sales Analytics Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project uses Microsoft Power BI to analyze Amazon sales data and present key business insights through an interactive dashboard.

The dashboard focuses on revenue, profit, quantity sold, profit margin, product performance, category-wise revenue, and state-wise profitability.

The objective is to understand sales performance, identify profitable products and regions, and support data-driven business decisions.

## Business Objectives

- Monitor total revenue and profit.
- Analyze the quantity of products sold.
- Evaluate overall profit margin.
- Identify top-performing products.
- Compare revenue across product categories.
- Analyze profitability across states.
- Track monthly revenue trends.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

| KPI | Description |
|---|---|
| Total Revenue | Total sales revenue generated |
| Total Profit | Total profit earned |
| Total Quantity Sold | Total quantity of products sold |
| Profit Margin | Profit as a percentage of revenue |

## Dashboard Features

### 1. KPI Cards
Displays total revenue, total profit, total quantity sold, and profit margin.

### 2. Monthly Revenue Analysis
A line chart shows revenue trends across the months and highlights changes in sales performance.

### 3. Product Performance Analysis
A horizontal bar chart compares revenue generated by individual products.

### 4. Category-Wise Revenue
A doughnut chart shows the contribution of different product categories to total revenue.

### 5. State-Wise Profit Analysis
A chart compares profitability across states to identify strong and weak regional performance.

### 6. Interactive Filters
Users can filter the dashboard by state, category, and month.

## Key Business Questions

- What is the total revenue and profit?
- How does revenue change from month to month?
- Which products generate the highest revenue?
- Which categories contribute the most to sales?
- Which states generate the highest profit?
- How can the business improve profitability?

## Business Recommendations

- Investigate top-performing products to identify opportunities for further growth.
- Review low-performing products to understand their sales challenges.
- Monitor monthly revenue trends to identify seasonal patterns.
- Compare state-wise profitability when planning regional sales strategies.
- Evaluate product category performance when making inventory and marketing decisions.
